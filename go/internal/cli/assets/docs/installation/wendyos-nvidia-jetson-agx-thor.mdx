Jetson AGX Thor uses a dedicated USB recovery flash path. WendyOS flashes the Thor's QSPI and internal NVMe directly; no external drive is required.

WendyOS on Jetson is built on **JetPack 7.2** (L4T R39.2.0). Apps can target JetPack 7.2 or later.

## What You Need

- NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor Developer Kit
- USB-C data cable for your MacBook or other development machine
- Supplied Thor power supply
- Wendy CLI installed on your development machine

<Steps>

    ### Connect Your Computer

    Turn the Thor so the **back faces you**; you should see all of the ports. Connect the other end to the **right USB-C port, next to HDMI**, labeled **To Computer** in the clip.

  <Step>
    ### Connect Power

    Plug the supplied power adapter into a power outlet, then connect its USB-C cable to the **left USB-C port** on the Thor, labeled **Power Cable** in the clip.

      <figcaption>Connect the supplied power adapter to the left USB-C port.</figcaption>
    </figure>
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Enter Recovery Mode

    Turn the Thor so the **front faces you**. Its three buttons, from left to right, are **Power**, **Force Recovery**, and **Reset**.

    1. Briefly press the **left Power** button to turn the Thor on.
    2. Press and **hold** the **middle Force Recovery** button.
    3. While holding it, briefly tap the **right Reset** button.
    4. Wait a moment, then release the **middle Force Recovery** button.

      <figcaption>The clip starts with the Thor powered on and shows the recovery-button sequence.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    In the next step, confirm that the installer detects the Thor in USB recovery mode before flashing.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Install WendyOS

    Run the installer from your terminal:

    ```bash
    wendy install
    ```

    Follow the on-screen instructions. The CLI downloads a Thor flashpack, detects the Thor in USB recovery mode, and flashes QSPI plus its internal NVMe.

    <figure>
      <video
        width="1920" height="1080" autoPlay loop muted playsInline controls preload="metadata"

        Your browser does not support embedded video.
      </video>
      <figcaption>The installer detects the Thor in USB recovery mode and shows flash progress.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    The recording uses `sudo wendy os install`. `wendy install` runs the same installer and requests elevated access when needed.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Boot WendyOS

    After the CLI reports a successful flash, power-cycle the Thor out of recovery mode. It will boot WendyOS from internal storage; leave the data-capable USB-C cable connected to your computer.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Discover Your Device

    Wait 1-2 minutes for WendyOS to boot, then run:

    ```bash
    wendy discover
    ```

    <img
      src={`${(process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_BASE_PATH || '').replace(/\/$/, '')}/images/docs/cli/discover/devices-thor-dark.png`}
      alt="The Wendy CLI listing a discovered NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor on the network"
      style={{ width: '100%' }}
    />

    Your Thor is ready for development. See [Discovering Devices](/docs/device/discovering-devices) to learn more.
  </Step>
</Steps>
