ROS 2 · 20 Hz

Drive controls

Grant this app control in the simulator, then enable this tab.

Use W / S to walk, A / D to sidestep, and Q / E to turn. Space or Escape disables controls. Touch and mouse work too.

Pace & requests

Forward0.00 m/s
Left0.00 m/s
Turn left0.00 rad/s
Heartbeat timeout250 ms

These are velocity requests. The robot moves only while the simulator grants this ROS publisher control. Open the sandbox alongside this page to see its movement.

Switching tabs, losing focus, or losing the connection disables this tab. Enable again to continue.

Manual driving has no obstacle avoidance. The server stops requests after 250 ms without a heartbeat, with a 50 ms publisher interval.
